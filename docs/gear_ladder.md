# Gear Ladder & Crafting Progression

> **Living Document:** Defines the equipment progression across dungeon floor bands, mapping enemy materials to craftable weapons, armor, and consumables. Keep this document updated whenever enemies, drops, or recipes change.

---

## 1. Overview & Band Structure

The game segments equipment crafting into **Floor Bands**, matching dungeon depth to economic progression:
- **Band 1 — Ancient Crypts (Floors 1–2):** 100% Core. Starter economy: wood, ore, bone, pelts, and bowstrings. Supplies the full tier-0 iron, hardwood, and leather gear ladder.
- **Band 2 — Abyssal Depths (Floors 3–5):** 70% Core / 30% Carry-over. Introduces subterranean Spider Silk, Venom, and Elite Orc materials (Steel Scrap, Heavy Hide). Crafts the steel, composite, and silk gear rungs.
- **Band 3 — Infernal Caldera (Floors 6–10):** 70% Core / 30% Carry-over. Introduces Epic Void Knight materials (Void Plate, Void Essence, Void Core) and pinnacle tier-1 voidforged weapons and heavy armor.
- **Band 4 — Glacial Caverns (Floors 11+):** 70% Core / 30% Carry-over. Introduces Boss Glacial materials (Rime Carapace, Glacial Core/Essence). Future home for boss-tier crafting rungs.

## 5. Station Recipe Levels & Hierarchy

All recipe levels follow the Director-approved tier structure:
- **Band 1 (Novice):** Levels 0–2.
  - Level 0: `short_sword`, `daggers`, `mace`, `iron_shield`, `lockpick`, `fishing_rod`, `smelt_broken_lockbox`, `leather_cap`, `leather_armor`, `quarterstaff`, `hunting_bow`, and all Alchemy.
  - Level 1: `longsword_1h`, `spear`, `crossbow`, `throwing_weapons`, `bone_necklace`.
  - Level 2: `katana`, `longsword_2h`, `spears_2h`, `wolf_claw_ring`.
- **Band 2 (Adept):** Levels 5–6.
  - Level 5: `greatsword`, `heavy_mace`, `steel_short_sword`, `steel_longsword`, `steel_katana`, `steel_spear`, `steel_halberd`, `serrated_daggers`, `heavy_crossbow`, `steel_shield`, `steel_throwing_weapons`, `composite_bow`, `reinforced_staff`, `silk_cowl`, `venom_charm`.
  - Level 6: `silk_robe`.
- **Band 3 (Pinnacle):** Level 8.
  - Level 8: `spiked_morningstar`, `war_bow`, all `voidforged` weapons, `void_greathelm`, `void_plate_armor`, `void_pendant`, `void_ring`, `void_relic`.
